Recipe #1 Unicorn Fudge

  • Time: 1 hour
  • Serves: 20 people
Ingredients Quantity
Bag of White Chocolate Chips 11 ounces
Can of Strawberry Frosting 16 ounces
Magical Sprinkles 1 cup

Step 1: Melt white chocolate chips in microwave safe bowl in 30 second increments.

Step 2: Stir in between each 30 second interval till smooth.

Step 3: Add in frosting and stir until well incorporated.

Step 4: Line 8×8 pan with plastic wrap.

Step 5: Pour mixture into pan and tap so even.

Step 6: Sprinkle magical sprinkles on top as and refrigerate for 1 hour.

Step 7: Take out of pan after chilled by pulling on edges of plastic wrap.

Step 8: Cut into small squares and enjoy!
